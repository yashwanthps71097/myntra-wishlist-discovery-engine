# Myntra Growth Team: User Interview Prep Guide

This guide is designed to help Product Managers validate the **Top 5-6 Purchase Barriers** identified by the AI Discovery Engine through **5–6 qualitative user interviews** (as part of Phase 5).

---

## 1. Participant Recruitment Criteria

To validate the findings effectively, recruit **5–6 participants** who match the following criteria:
*   **Active Myntra Users:** Have logged into the Myntra app at least 3 times in the last 30 days.
*   **High-Intent Wishlist Users:** Currently have **10+ items wishlisted** for more than 20 days.
*   **Low Conversion Cohort:** Have not purchased any item from their wishlist in the last 30 days.

---

## 2. Interview Structure & Timeline (45 Mins Total)

| Section | Duration | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intro & Warm-up** | 5 mins | Establish rapport; understand general fashion shopping habits. |
| **Wishlist Walkthrough** | 15 mins | Ask the user to share their screen, open their Myntra wishlist, and walk through why they saved specific items. |
| **Deep Dive: Roadblocks** | 20 mins | Probe on the specific barriers (Price, Sizing, Stock) using the prompts below. |
| **Wrap-up** | 5 mins | Collect feedback on potential dashboard MVP ideas (e.g. price tracker). |

---

## 3. Targeted Interview Prompts by Barrier

### Barrier A: Price Uncertainty (Opportunity Score: ~26.0)
*   *Hypothesis:* Value seekers postpone checkout because they lack baseline price confidence and wait for recurring EORS events.
*   **Questions to Ask:**
    1.  *Walk me through this wishlisted dress. How long has it been here, and what is holding you back from clicking "Buy Now"?*
    2.  *How do you determine if a discount on Myntra is a "good deal" or if you should wait longer?*
    3.  *Have you ever added an item to your cart during a sale only to remove it at checkout? What changed?*

### Barrier B: Fit or Size Confidence (Opportunity Score: ~17.0)
*   *Hypothesis:* Users postpone clothing purchases out of anxiety about size charts and return hassles.
*   **Questions to Ask:**
    1.  *Have you ever kept a garment wishlisted because you weren't sure how the sizing fits (e.g. Roadster shirts vs. Zara)?*
    2.  *How do you use other customers' height and weight reviews to decide on a size?*
    3.  *What was your last return experience like on Myntra? How does that affect your purchase speed today?*

### Barrier C: Out of Stock (Opportunity Score: ~9.0)
*   *Hypothesis:* Popular wishlist items sell out in target sizes before users receive price drop notifications.
*   **Questions to Ask:**
    1.  *How often do you check back to find that an item in your wishlist has gone "Out of Stock"?*
    2.  *If an item is sold out, do you look for similar designs on Myntra, or do you leave the app?*
    3.  *Would you pay a small reservation deposit to secure your size for up to 48 hours?*
